The Short Version

Three Features Exclusive to Zooroll.

Privacy

Zero Network Uploads

Zooroll requests no network permissions, guaranteeing that your recordings never leave your local device. Unlike cloud-first recorders, Zooroll is safe for capturing customer data, internal dashboards, and NDA-restricted materials.

Pointing

The Fading Blinker Tool

Click anywhere while recording to generate a fading spotlight pulse. Point out specific UI elements without cluttering your screen with permanent drawings or static shapes.

Audio Filter

Integrated Microphone Noise Gate

Zooroll applies an active audio noise gate to suppress steady background noise (fans, air conditioning) and silences background mic noise between spoken words.

Where Other Recorders Perform Better

An honest assessment of when to choose an alternative tool over Zooroll:

  • Instant Shareable Links: Tools like Loom, Vidyard, and Vimeo Record auto-upload recordings to generate instant sharing links. Zooroll exports direct local video files.
  • In-Browser Video Editing: Competitors like Screencastify provide native video trimming and cutting features. Zooroll focuses strictly on capture.
  • AI Captions & Transcripts: Loom and Vidyard automatically generate closed captions and searchable transcripts.
  • Open Source Auditability: Screenity is open-source and local-first, suitable for developers who require fully auditable source code.
